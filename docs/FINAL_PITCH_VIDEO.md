## 📹 3. Owner Recording Instructions & Shot List

### 🖥️ Display & Environment Setup
- **Resolution:** 1920 x 1080 (1080p, 16:9 aspect ratio).
- **Browser:** Chrome or Brave in fullscreen mode (Hide bookmarks bar, extension icons, personal tabs).
- **Audio:** Clear microphone audio without background noise.
- **Mouse Cursor:** Move cursor deliberately; pause 1-2 seconds when highlighting key badges (`HIRABLE`, `AgentProof`).

### 🎬 Shot List (Single-Take Recording Path)
1. **Shot 1 (0:00 - 0:35):** Start at `https://agentflow-bnb.vercel.app/`. Scroll hero section to show 290k+ badge.
2. **Shot 2 (0:35 - 0:55):** Click **Rebalancing** category card to view `/categories/rebalancing`.
3. **Shot 3 (0:55 - 1:20):** Click **Explore** to open `/agents`. Toggle **HIRABLE ONLY** filter badge.
4. **Shot 4 (1:20 - 1:40):** Click **Compare** on 2 agents and launch `/compare`.
5. **Shot 5 (1:40 - 2:05):** Navigate to `/agents/56/49637` to highlight **AgentProof Independent Evidence**.
6. **Shot 6 (2:05 - 2:35):** Click **Hire Agent** to launch `ERC8183HireWizardModal`. Step through Task ➔ Quote ➔ Review ➔ Wallet.
7. **Shot 7 (2:35 - 2:45):** Close modal and return to Homepage hero.
